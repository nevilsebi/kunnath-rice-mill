Common Questions

Wholesale, NRI & Ordering FAQs

Why do Chilli Powder and Coriander Powder show "Live Market Rate"?

Whole dried chilli and coriander prices fluctuate regularly in the Thrissur spice market. Rather than locking in an inflated price, we confirm the exact daily rate with you on WhatsApp before you pay.

How do you pack orders for NRI international flights?

We mill your order fresh, pack it in thick food-grade pouches with double heat-sealing to prevent leaks or aroma escape in check-in luggage, and label each pack clearly for customs inspection.

Can NRIs order monthly groceries for parents living in Kerala?

Yes. Simply select "Thrissur Local Delivery" or "Pan-India Courier", enter your family's address in the WhatsApp confirmation, and we will deliver freshly milled packs directly to their doorstep.

Do you offer bulk rates for catering, weddings, and restaurants?

Yes. For orders of 10 kg and above per item, fill out the Wholesale & Bulk Quote form below or message us on WhatsApp for special bulk milling rates.
